SNAP VANISH — INSTALL (about 1 minute)
=======================================

1. Unzip snap-vanish.zip. You get a folder called "snap-vanish".
   Put it somewhere permanent (e.g. Documents) — Chrome loads the extension
   from this folder, so don't delete it.

2. In Chrome, go to:  chrome://extensions

3. Turn on "Developer mode" (switch in the top-right corner).

4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the "snap-vanish" folder
   (the one that contains manifest.json).

5. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next
   to "Snap Vanish".

6. Open your call in the browser — Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or Zoom
   (web). If the call tab was already open, reload it once.
   Turn your camera on. After a few seconds you'll see
   "Snap Vanish is ready" in the bottom-left corner.

7. Snap your fingers where the camera can see your hand.
   You turn to dust, the room behind you stays, and your mic mutes.
   Snap again to come back (mic unmutes).

   Backup: press Alt+Shift+S, or click "Snap now" in the Snap Vanish popup.


TIPS
----
- Snap with your hand clearly in view, palm roughly facing the camera,
  20–60 cm from it. Hold the "ready to snap" pose for a moment, then snap.
- For the best empty room, lean or move around a little in the first
  minute: every bit of room the camera sees is remembered, and only the
  parts it has never seen are filled in by the AI.
- Turn off the meeting app's own background blur/replace effects.
- "Saw your snap but didn't hear it": just snap once more — two clear snaps
  in a row always count.
- Too many false snaps? Lower "Snap sensitivity" in the popup.
  Snaps not noticed? Raise it, or untick "Confirm snaps with the click sound".
- Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts

Everything runs on your computer. No video or audio is sent anywhere by
Snap Vanish.
